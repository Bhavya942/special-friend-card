DIL ki baatein 💕

Tum meri life ki wo ladki ho jo sirf sunti nahi, balki mujhe poori tarah samajhti bhi ho. Main maanta hoon ki main kabhi-kabhi zyada bol deta hoon, overthink karta hoon, aur apni har chhoti baat tumhare saamne rakh deta hoon. Par tumhari sabse khaas baat ye hai ki tum hamesha patience ke saath mujhe sunti ho, bina judge kiye, bina thake. Tumhari baaton me ek alag hi sukoon hota hai. Jab main tension me hota hoon, tumhara calm nature mujhe automatically strong feel karwata hai. Mujhe ye bahut pasand hai ki tum sirf “sab theek ho jayega” nahi kehti, balki tumhari baaton se sach me sab theek hone lagta hai, jaise tumhari presence hi solution ho. Tum bahut caring ho, bina kisi dikhawa ke. Tumhari honesty, tumhara support aur tumhara understanding nature mujhe tumse aur bhi zyada connect kar deta hai. Sach kahun to, mujhe tumhari aadat si ho gayi hai — tumhari baatein, tumhara saath, aur tumhari simplicity. Isliye aaj dil se poochna chahta hoon — kya tum meri zindagi ka wo hissa banogi jahan main apna har kal, har khushi aur har struggle tumhare saath share kar sakun? 🌷💫
