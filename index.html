DIL ki baata

Tum meri life ki wo friend ho jo sirf sunती nahi, balki samajhti bhi ho. Main maanta hoon ki main kabhi-kabhi zyada bol deta hoon, zyada overthink karta hoon, aur har chhoti baat tumhare saamne rakh deta hoon. Par tumhari sabse badi quality ye hai ki tum kabhi irritate nahi hoti. Tum itne patience ke saath meri baat sunti ho jaise tum already solution dhoond rahi ho.

Tumhari baaton me ek alag hi shanti hoti hai. Jab main tension me hota hoon, tab tumhara calm nature mujhe automatically relaxed feel karwata hai. Mujhe sabse zyada ye pasand hai ki tum sirf “sab theek ho jayega” nahi kehti, balki sach me 1–2 din me cheezein theek ho jaati hain, jaise tumhari baaton me koi magic ho.

Tum bahut caring ho, bina dikhawa ke. Tumhari honesty, tumhara support, aur tumhara understanding nature tumhe special banata hai. Tum na sirf meri friend ho, balki wo insaan ho jiske saamne main bina soche apni har baat keh sakta hoon.

Sach bolun to, tumhari friendship meri life ke strongest pillars me se ek hai. Tum jaisi ho, waisi hi rehna—kyunki tumhari simplicity aur maturity hi tumhari sabse badi tareef hai 🌷💫
